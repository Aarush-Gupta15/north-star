Privacy by design — not just a promise

Your text is analysed entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account, no tracking. Nothing to leak, because nothing is ever sent.

Detection is a bundled JavaScript engine. The only thing that can ever leave — and only if you explicitly turn it on — is an anonymous summary (types & counts, never values) to your own dashboard.

⚖️ Built for the world's toughest privacy laws

Compliant with the DPDP Act, 2023 & DPDP Rules, 2025

Most tools react after a leak. North Star is built to prevent one — which is exactly what modern data-protection law asks of you. It maps directly to the core duties under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 14 Nov 2025), and aligns with the EU GDPR.

Questions

Is any sensitive or confidential data stored anywhere?

No. North Star never stores your sensitive or confidential data — not on your device, and not on any server. Detection runs in memory inside your browser and the text is discarded the moment it's checked. In the Organization edition, the only thing that can be recorded is a masked summary (data types and counts, plus a last-4 snippet like card •••• 1111) on the company's own backend — the actual values are never captured, transmitted, or saved. You can't leak what was never stored.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All detection happens in your browser with a bundled engine. There's no backend and no account. The optional team sync transmits only a summary (types and counts), never the actual text.

Does this help with India's DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025?

Yes — that's a core part of why it exists. North Star enforces data minimisation and privacy by design by keeping sensitive personal data from leaving your device, and it recognises the exact identifiers India's DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 14 Nov 2025) are meant to protect (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, and more). The Organization edition adds audit trails and PDF compliance digests — supporting duties like the 72-hour breach reporting window — so a company can demonstrate accountability. It also aligns with the EU GDPR. It's a safeguard that supports compliance — not legal advice or a certification.

Which sites does it work on?

Every website in Chrome — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, WhatsApp Web, Gmail, forms, anywhere you type. (Native phone/desktop apps are out of scope for a browser extension.)

Will it flag normal text?

It's tuned to avoid false alarms — checksums for IDs and cards, and a symbol-aware heuristic for passwords, so everyday words and product names don't trigger it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The personal extension is free and open-source. A paid team tier is planned for organisations that need audit and policy controls.
