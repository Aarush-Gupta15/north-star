Privacy Policy

North Star — Privacy Guard · Last updated: 9 July 2026

North Star is built around a single principle: your data stays on your device.

The short version: North Star does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There is no server and no account. All detection happens locally in your browser, and your text is discarded the moment it's checked.

What the extension does

North Star scans text you type, paste, or select on web pages to detect sensitive personal information — such as Aadhaar, PAN, credit/debit cards, passwords, API keys, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and health-related terms — and warns you before you share it. All detection runs locally, in-memory, inside your browser.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • We do not send the text you type, or the pages you visit, to any server — there is no server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, cookies, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not have a backend of any kind. Detection is a JavaScript engine bundled inside the extension.
  • Your text is processed in memory to check it, then discarded. It is never written to disk or persisted.

Data we store locally

The extension saves only your settings using Chrome's storage API — your on/off toggles, your own custom rules, and your per-site off-list. This stays within your browser (and, if you use Chrome Sync, your own Google profile). It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Regulatory alignment

North Star is designed as a data-protection control that helps you meet your obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, and to align with the EU GDPR — by keeping sensitive personal data on your device (data minimisation and privacy by design/by default) and never storing raw personal data.

North Star is a safeguard that supports compliance; it is not legal advice or a certification, and data-controller / Data Fiduciary responsibilities remain with you or your organisation.

Children's privacy

North Star is a general-purpose privacy tool and is not directed at children.
